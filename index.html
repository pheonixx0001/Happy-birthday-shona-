A Letter For You 💌

Some wishes are too special to fit into just one birthday message.

Dear Shona Bhabhi,

First of all, a very, very happy birthday to you! ❤️ I hope your special day brings you countless reasons to smile, lots of love, beautiful surprises and everything that makes your heart happy.

I just want to say that having you as my bhabhi is genuinely something special. You are not just a relation in the family; you are someone who brings warmth, happiness and a lovely kind of positivity with you. Your presence makes things feel a little more cheerful, and your smile has its own way of making everyone's day better.

I may not always express it properly, and sometimes I might even tease you for absolutely no reason, but please know that you are truly valued and respected. You deserve all the love, care and happiness in the world. I hope you never have to compromise on your dreams, your happiness or the little things that make you feel like yourself.

May this new year of your life open beautiful doors for you. May every effort you make bring you closer to your dreams, every difficult day turn into a lesson, and every ordinary day give you a special reason to be grateful. I wish you good health, peace of mind, success and endless moments that make you genuinely happy.

And one more thing, Shona Bhabhi — keep smiling, keep being your lovely self, and never forget how important you are to this family. You deserve to be celebrated not just today, but every single day.

Once again, happiest birthday! May your life always be filled with beautiful blessings, sweet surprises and people who make you feel as special as you truly are. Lots of respect, affection and best wishes from your devar. 🌷

With lots of love & respect,
Your devar ♡
