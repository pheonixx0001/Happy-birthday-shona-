A Letter From Your Devar 💌

Some feelings deserve more than a simple happy birthday message.

Dear Shona Bhabhi, 🌷

Happiest birthday to you, Shona Bhabhi! ❤️ I hope today brings you a thousand little reasons to smile and reminds you just how special you are. I wanted to make something a little different for your birthday, something that could put a smile on your face and make you feel extra loved and appreciated.

Honestly, having you as my bhabhi is a lovely part of my life. You are not just someone who became a part of our family; you are someone whose presence adds its own warmth and happiness. I hope you always feel welcomed, respected and appreciated, because you genuinely deserve all of that and so much more.

I know I might tease you sometimes, annoy you with silly jokes or act like the most mischievous devar ever. But that's just how I show my little bit of affection! 😂 Behind every bit of that teasing is a lot of respect and genuine care. And yes, you will have to tolerate your devar's nonsense for a long time!

On your birthday, I wish that every dream you have ever imagined gets a chance to come true. May you achieve everything you work hard for, may your efforts always be recognised, and may you never have to give up on the things that make you happy. I hope life surprises you with beautiful opportunities and gives you countless reasons to feel proud of yourself.

More than anything, I wish you good health, peace of mind and genuine happiness. May you always have people around you who understand you, support you and stand beside you. May your difficult days become easier and your happy days become even more beautiful. You deserve a life full of kindness and blessings.

And please, never stop smiling. Keep being the lovely person you are. Never underestimate your importance in this family or the happiness your presence brings. I might not say these things every day, but I truly wish the best for you.

So enjoy your special day to the fullest. Eat your favourite food, accept all the pampering, take lots of pictures and don't forget to save some birthday cake for your favourite devar! 😂🎂

Once again, happiest birthday, Shona Bhabhi. May this new chapter of your life be filled with beautiful beginnings, endless blessings, lots of laughter and all the happiness your heart can hold. Stay blessed always! 💗

With lots of love & respect,
Your devar ♡
